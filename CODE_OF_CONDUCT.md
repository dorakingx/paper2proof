# Code of Conduct

Paper2Proof contributors are expected to make participation respectful,
welcoming, and safe regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Be constructive, assume good intent while responding to concrete impact, cite
evidence, respect privacy and licensing, and accept correction. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, or deliberate disruption
are not acceptable in project spaces.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior undermines these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately
to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly
as practical; retaliation is prohibited.

This concise policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and applies to
repository discussions, reviews, issues, and project-related public spaces.
